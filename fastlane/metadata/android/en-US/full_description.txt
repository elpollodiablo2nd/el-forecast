<p>elspot forecasts Danish electricity spot prices (elspot) for the DK1 and DK2
price areas, 24/48/72 hours ahead, with a full uncertainty curve (21 quantiles)
per hour instead of a single point estimate.</p>

<p>The forecast runs fully on the phone: Mistral's open Timestral time-series
foundation model is exported to ONNX and executed with onnxruntime-web (WASM)
inside the app &mdash; no API key and no server round-trip for the model. The 5M
model is bundled in the APK; the more accurate 28M model is downloaded once and
then kept in the app cache.</p>

<p>Prices and grid fees come from Energi Data Service (Energinet, keyless), the
weather from Open-Meteo. A bundled price snapshot is the last-resort source when
the APIs rate-limit, so previously loaded prices keep working offline.</p>

<p>No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The only permission requested is INTERNET
(to fetch prices, weather and the optional 28M model).</p>

<p>Source code: https://github.com/elpollodiablo2nd/el-forecast &mdash; the APK
is built reproducibly from source (see FDROID.md).</p>
